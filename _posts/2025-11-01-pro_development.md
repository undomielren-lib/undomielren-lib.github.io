## Presentations & Panels

- **LOUD 2026 (Panelist):** *"AI in Libraries & Higher Education"*. Discussed the impact of AI and generative AI content on information and digital literacy.

- **Love Data Week 2026:** *"Concepts of Data-Driven Visualization"*.
